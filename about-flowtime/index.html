About Flowtime

What is Flowtime?

Flowtime is an ADHD focus timer for time blindness, hyperfocus, Pomodoro, focus reminders, project time tracking, and optional app blocking on iPhone and Android. It is designed around ADHD time blindness, hyperfocus, and reducing distracting context switches. No account is required.

What Flowtime does

Flowtime helps make focused time visible. You can start a standard timer, use Pomodoro, create a flexible Flow Session with a target duration, receive focus reminders, track time by project, and review session history and reports.

Flowtime also includes optional app blocking where available, so distracting apps can be made less available during intentional focus periods.

  • Focus timer modes
  • Time reminders
  • App blocking guide

Why Flowtime exists

Flowtime was created by Ava Thalheim from personal experience with ADHD. Getting focused is not always the problem. Losing track of time, staying in hyperfocus too long, or stopping at a reasonable point can also be difficult.

The app is designed to create visible and session boundaries around focused time, without forcing every session into one rigid timer rhythm.

Privacy and accounts

No account is required to use Flowtime. The privacy policy states that timer and project data remain on your device and are not sent to Flowtime servers.

Flowtime does not describe cloud sync on the website. Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. The website uses privacy-friendly Plausible analytics, and the app privacy policy states that optional app-blocking accessibility data is only used to provide app blocking and is not used for advertising, analytics, or personal data collection.

Read the Flowtime privacy policy.

Who created Flowtime?

Flowtime was created by Ava Thalheim. The public creator website is avathalheim.dev.

What Flowtime is not

Flowtime is not a medical product, diagnostic tool, or ADHD treatment. It is a focus and time-awareness tool built around practical experiences with distraction, time blindness, and hyperfocus.
