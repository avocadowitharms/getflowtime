About Flowtime

SHORT DESCRIPTION

Flowtime is a flexible focus timer and time tracker for iOS and Android. Built with a local-first philosophy, it combines Pomodoro, open-ended Flow Sessions, gentle reminders, and app shielding without requiring accounts or cloud sync.

LONG DESCRIPTION

Flowtime offers an intentional alternative to rigid interval timers and heavy enterprise tracking tools. Designed around bold Swiss typography and friction-free interaction, Flowtime lets users track deep work sessions, view focus trends, and protect their attention without clutter or distractions.
