About Flowtime

Editorial overview ready for quotation or background context.

Flowtime is a minimal focus timer built around a simple idea: starting a focus session should require almost no effort.

Instead of adding complicated workflows, accounts, or productivity systems, Flowtime removes friction between deciding to work and actually starting.

The app combines flexible focus timers with awareness reminders, optional app shielding, focus history, and tools designed around problems such as time blindness and hyperfocus.

Why Flowtime Exists

The founder story behind the app.

Flowtime started as a personal tool.

Its creator, Swiss indie developer Ava Thalheim, wanted a focus timer that helped with both sides of focused work: starting when it feels difficult, and stopping when hyperfocus makes hours disappear.

The result became Flowtime — a deliberately simple focus timer built around awareness rather than punishment.

Ava Thalheim
Creator of Flowtime

What Makes Flowtime Different

Key design philosophy and feature principles.

One-tap focus sessions

Start tracking focused time instantly without setup wizards, onboarding surveys, or mandatory tag configurations.

No account required

Launch the app and start focusing immediately. Your session data remains strictly stored on your device.

Privacy-first design

No ad tracking, no monetization algorithms, and no personal time data stored on external servers.

Flexible timer modes

Choose open count-up timers, classic Pomodoro, or open-ended Flow Sessions tailored to your current energy level.

Awareness reminders for time blindness and hyperfocus

Gentle audio and visual check-in cues make time visible while working so hours don't slip away unnoticed.

Optional app shielding

Interrupt attention-hijacking apps with conscious pause screens rather than aggressive, punitive lockout mechanics.

Medium description (~150 words) 
Flowtime is an intentional, privacy-first focus timer designed around time blindness and hyperfocus. Unlike traditional productivity tools that add heavy workflows, accounts, and setup friction, Flowtime focuses on making focus sessions effortless to start and easy to maintain. Users can choose between standard timers, Pomodoro, and open-ended Flow Sessions with custom target durations. To address hyperfocus and time blindness, Flowtime provides gentle check-in reminders that keep time visible while working. For digital distractions, optional app shielding places a conscious barrier in front of attention-hijacking apps. Flowtime is built local-first: timer and project data stay on your device, no account is required, and privacy is respected by design. Available on the App Store for iPhone and Google Play for Android.
Founder bio 
Ava Thalheim is an indie software developer based in Switzerland. Experiencing the daily challenges of time blindness and hyperfocus, she built Flowtime to create a simple, respectful focus timer that makes focused time easier to notice and manage. Learn more at avathalheim.dev.
