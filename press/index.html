BEHIND THE APP

Built to make
time visible.

Its creator, Swiss indie developer Ava Thalheim, wanted a focus timer that helped with both sides of focused work: starting when it feels difficult, and stopping when hyperfocus makes hours disappear.

The result became Flowtime — a deliberately simple focus timer built around awareness rather than punishment.

Product overview

Flowtime is a flexible, friction-free focus timer and personal time tracker for iPhone and Android. It combines open-ended Flow Sessions, Pomodoro, focus reminders, projects, session history, reports, and optional app blocking. Flowtime does not require an account, and timer and project data are stored locally on the device.
