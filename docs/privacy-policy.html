Privacy Policy Flowtime respects your privacy. Flowtime is designed as a simple, privacy-friendly time tracking app. The app stores your projects, time entries, settings, and tracking data locally on your device. We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not track you across apps or websites. Data Stored Locally Flowtime may store the following information on your device: - Projects you create - Time tracking sessions - Focus mode settings - App preferences - Pro purchase status This data is stored locally on your device and is not sent to our servers. Purchases Flowtime may use Apple’s in-app purchase system to process Flowtime Pro purchases. Purchase processing is handled by Apple. Data Sharing Flowtime does not share your personal data with third parties for advertising or tracking purposes.
