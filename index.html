Frequently Asked Questions

What is Flowtime?

Flowtime is a minimalist focus timer and time tracker for iPhone and Android. It supports a standard timer, Flow Sessions, Pomodoro, projects, reminders, history, reports, and privacy-first personal productivity.

Who is Flowtime for?

Flowtime is for students, freelancers, developers, writers, researchers, and anyone who wants simple personal time tracking without managing a heavy workspace.

Is Flowtime good for ADHD?

Flowtime can help people who benefit from external time cues, gentle reminders, visible sessions, and low-friction project switching. It is not a medical tool.

How is Flowtime different from Pomodoro?

Flowtime includes Pomodoro, but also supports open-ended Flow Sessions, standard time tracking, project selection, reminders, and reviewable focus history.

How is Flowtime different from Forest, Toggl, or Session?

Flowtime is less gamified than Forest, lighter than team-oriented tools like Toggl, and more cross-platform than Apple-first timers such as Session.
