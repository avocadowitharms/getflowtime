10WHY FLOWTIME EXISTS

Why was Flowtime created?

Ava, the founder of Flowtime, has ADHD. During intense hyperfocus, she can spend weeks mentally stuck on work, features, UI details, or whatever her brain has decided matters. Even when she stops working physically, her brain often keeps going. That experience influenced Flowtime, a flexible focus timer and time tracker for anyone.

Flowtime began as a way to focus longer. Over time, it also became a way to unfocus on purpose. Sometimes Ava starts a one-hour FlowSession, blocks distracting apps, and goes for a walk with her dog. Knowing the timer is running and the usual apps are unavailable makes it easier for her to be present instead of repeatedly checking her phone or thinking about work.

All questions & answers ↗
What is Flowtime?

Flowtime is a flexible focus timer and personal time tracker for iPhone and Android. No account is required.

Who is Flowtime for?

For work, study, creative projects, and anyone who wants to see where their time goes. Especially when starting, stopping, or noticing time doesn’t come easily.

Is it just a Pomodoro timer?

Pomodoro is one option. Classic counts up, Flowmodoro bases breaks on your focus time, and Flow Sessions let you choose a target without rigid intervals.

Which languages does the app support?

English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Dutch, Polish, and Portuguese.

Do I need an account?

No. Open Flowtime and start. Timer and project data stay on your device.
