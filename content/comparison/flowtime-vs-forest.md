## Summary

Flowtime and Forest offer different approaches to keeping your focus. Forest relies on gamification and visual rewards like planting virtual trees, while Flowtime provides a clean, distraction-free utility timer focused on projects and clean reports without game mechanics.

## Quick Comparison

| Criteria | Flowtime | Forest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focus Method | Straightforward timer & logs | Gamified tree growth (fails if you leave app) |
| Interface | Minimalist, dark/mono themes | Illustrative cartoon forest |
| Data Model | Local-first, private | Account-based, sync-heavy |
| Tracking Style | Flexible open-ended flow or Pomodoro | Strict countdown timers |
| Reporting | Detailed weekly/monthly charts | Visual forests and achievement history |

## Choose Flowtime If

You want a focused timer that tracks your work without gamifying it. Flowtime is ideal if you like to log time spent on actual projects, need flexible open-ended sessions, and prefer a distraction-free, minimalist user interface that stays out of your way.

## Choose Forest If

You struggle with staying off your phone and find value in visual stakes. Forest's mechanism of killing a virtual tree when you close the app can provide the necessary behavioral nudge if you enjoy collecting achievements.

## FAQ

### Does Flowtime gamify focus sessions?

No, Flowtime does not use game mechanics, virtual currencies, or achievements. It is a utility-first focus tool.

### What platforms do they support?

Both Flowtime and Forest are mobile focus apps. Flowtime is available on iOS and Android, whereas Forest is available on iOS, Android, and as a browser extension.
