## Summary

Flowtime and Be Focused are built to log your productive hours. Be Focused is built around standard interval tracking (often tied to specific task titles), whereas Flowtime allows you to choose between strict classic intervals and adaptive open-ended Flow sessions with automatic project tag categorization.

## Quick Comparison

| Criteria | Flowtime | Be Focused |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Session Style | Open-ended Flow & classic intervals | Classic interval blocks (Pomodoro) |
| Interface | Modern, distraction-free | Simple menu bar / desktop app |
| Customization | Mono/classic themes | Customizable sounds & intervals |
| Platform Support | iOS and Android | macOS and iOS focus |
| Focus Reporting | Distribution charts, tagging | History logs |

## Choose Flowtime If

You prefer flexibility in how you work. With support for both classic intervals and open-ended Flow sessions, Flowtime adapts to your natural workflow rather than forcing you to take breaks when you are in a state of high productivity.

## Choose Be Focused If

You want a simple desktop utility on macOS that sits in your menu bar and sends standard chime notifications when your set interval completes.

## FAQ

### Does Be Focused support open-ended timers?

Be Focused is primarily structured around fixed intervals (e.g., 25-minute work and 5-minute break cycles).

### Can I export my data from Flowtime?

Yes, Flowtime stores all tracking logs on your device and provides options to export your raw session data.
