## Summary

Be Focused is a straightforward Pomodoro timer. Flowtime shares the preference for simplicity but adds more flexibility through open-ended sessions, project tracking, reminders, and personal reports.

## Quick comparison

| Criteria | Flowtime | Be Focused |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Session style | Open-ended Flow sessions or intervals | Fixed Pomodoro intervals |
| Best for | Flexible focus and time awareness | Classic Pomodoro routines |
| Project tracking | Built around projects and history | More task and interval oriented |
| Reminders | Check-ins for focus awareness | Timer alerts and interval sounds |
| Reports | Personal session reports | History and timer logs |
| Workflow | Adapts to variable session lengths | Encourages fixed work-break cycles |

## What Be Focused does well

Be Focused is appealing because it is direct. If you want a classic Pomodoro timer, it gives you the structure: work for a set amount of time, take a short break, repeat.

That structure helps many people. Fixed intervals can reduce procrastination, make work feel approachable, and create regular breaks.

If you already know that Pomodoro works for you, a dedicated timer like Be Focused may be enough.

## What Flowtime does differently

Flowtime does not assume every task should fit the same interval. Some sessions are short. Some take much longer. Sometimes a break is exactly what you need. Other times, a fixed break arrives right when you finally have momentum.

Flowtime supports more flexible tracking. You can use structured intervals when they help, or open-ended Flow sessions when the work needs room.

It also focuses on reviewing where your time went, not only completing cycles.

## Choose Be Focused if

Choose Be Focused if you love Pomodoro, fixed work and break cycles help you stay productive, and you want a dedicated timer with a familiar structure.

It may be the better choice if the main thing you need is a simple interval app.

## Choose Flowtime if

Choose Flowtime if your work sessions vary in length, you want both tracking and focus support, you care about project history, or you prefer reminders that create awareness instead of strict breaks.

It may be the better choice if you want Pomodoro sometimes, but not as the only way to work.

## Common mistakes when comparing them

One mistake is treating Pomodoro as a universal rule. It is a useful technique, but it is not the only way people focus.

Another mistake is ignoring session history. Completing intervals can feel good, but reviewing where the time went can teach you different things.

## A practical decision test

Think about what usually goes wrong in your work sessions. If the problem is starting, fixed intervals may help. A 25-minute block lowers the emotional cost of beginning, and a planned break can make the task feel less endless.

If the problem is interruption during deep work, fixed intervals may be less useful. You might need a longer runway, especially for coding, writing, research, or design. In that case, an open-ended timer with gentle reminders can preserve momentum while still helping you notice the time.

It can also be worth using both modes at different times. Use structured intervals for admin, chores, or low-energy starts. Use flexible sessions when the work needs continuity. The best timer is the one that fits the task in front of you.

## What to watch after choosing

After a week, review whether the timer helped you work or mostly made you negotiate with the timer. Fixed intervals should make starting easier and breaks more natural. Flexible sessions should make focus feel less interrupted while still giving you useful checkpoints.

If you keep ignoring a timer, the interval may not fit the work. If you keep overworking through flexible sessions, you may need stronger reminders. The right setup is the one that improves awareness without adding daily friction.

## FAQ

### Is Be Focused a Pomodoro app?

Yes. Be Focused is built around fixed work and break intervals.

### Does Flowtime support Pomodoro?

Flowtime supports structured focus sessions, while also allowing open-ended Flow sessions for work that does not fit a fixed interval.

### Which is better for long work sessions?

Flowtime is usually better if you want flexible, open-ended sessions. Be Focused is better if you prefer fixed cycles.

### Which app is simpler?

Both are simple in different ways. Be Focused is simple as an interval timer. Flowtime is simple as a flexible focus and time tracker.

If fixed intervals help sometimes but not always, Flowtime gives you a more flexible way to track focused work without giving up structure entirely.
