## Summary

Flowtime and Session are both modern focus trackers. Session is heavily integrated into the Apple ecosystem with calendar syncing and scripting support, while Flowtime offers an offline-first, cross-platform experience centered on pure project logs and clean dashboard statistics.

## Quick Comparison

| Criteria | Flowtime | Session |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platforms | iOS and Android | macOS, iOS, iPadOS only |
| Integrations | None (Fully local & manual) | Apple Calendar, Slack, Scripting |
| Account Model | No-account local storage | Cloud sync required (Apple ID) |
| Core Feature | Open Flow Sessions & Pomodoro | Strict focus blocks with session notes |
| Pricing | Free (Pro upgrade available) | Subscription-based Pro plan |

## Choose Flowtime If

You prefer an instant, account-free focus tracking setup on your iOS or Android device. Flowtime is a great fit for individuals who want clean distraction-free styling and local data storage without subscription fees.

## Choose Session If

You are fully invested in the Apple ecosystem, want your calendar events automatically converted to focus logs, and enjoy automation triggers (like updating your Slack status or running shortcuts when a timer starts).

## FAQ

### Does Flowtime support Apple Calendar sync?

No, Flowtime does not sync with external calendars. It functions as a standalone focus timer and manual project logger.

### Can I use Flowtime offline?

Yes, Flowtime is fully functional offline, storing all timer data and project histories directly on your device.
