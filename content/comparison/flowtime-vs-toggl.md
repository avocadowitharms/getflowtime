## Summary

Flowtime and Toggl solve different problems. Flowtime is built for personal focus tracking with minimal setup. Toggl is a broader time tracking platform for reporting, clients, and teams.

## Quick Comparison

| Criteria | Flowtime | Toggl |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Personal focus sessions | Team and client time tracking |
| Account required | No account-first workflow | Account-based workflow |
| Offline-first mindset | Yes | Depends on platform and sync needs |
| Reporting depth | Focused personal reports | Detailed business reporting |
| Setup complexity | Low | Medium |

## Choose Flowtime If

You want a minimalist timer for focused work, Pomodoro sessions, Flow Sessions, projects, and history without turning time tracking into a management system.

## Choose Toggl If

You need invoicing workflows, team reporting, client dashboards, workspace management, or integrations with a broader business stack.

## FAQ

### Is Flowtime a Toggl replacement?

For personal focus tracking, it can be. For team billing and client reporting, Toggl is more specialized.

### Which is better for deep work?

Flowtime is intentionally lighter and more focused on individual deep work.
